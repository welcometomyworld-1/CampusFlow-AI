# APEX TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY
## Academic Regulations & Attendance Policy (Clause 4.2)

### 1. Minimum Attendance Threshold
Under University Academic Ordinance Section 4.2:
- Every registered student must maintain an aggregate attendance of **at least 75%** across lectures, tutorials, and laboratories in each registered course to be eligible to sit for semester examinations.
- Attendance is calculated up to the last instructional working day prior to exams.

### 2. Warning and Condonation Range (70% - 74%)
- Students whose aggregate attendance falls between **70.0% and 74.9%** are placed on **"Academic Attendance Warning"**.
- Condonation may be granted by the Academic Dean strictly upon submission of verified medical certificates or pre-approved institutional representation records.
- Students in this band are required to attend mandatory remediation sessions and must not miss any remaining laboratory sessions or midterm assessments.

### 3. Critical Debarment (< 70%)
- Students with attendance below **70.0%** are deemed debarred from the end-semester examination and must repeat the course during the supplementary or summer term.
